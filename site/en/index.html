The HUD shows up only when you hesitate

What you see depends on how quickly you touch the trackpad after pressing the key: speed for people who know their layout, reassurance for people who are still learning. The trackpad is split into a 3×3 grid, and the button is chosen by where your finger rests.

Build and test in Xcode, mute and share your screen in Zoom. The nine buttons follow the app in front, and popular apps come with a ready-made layout.

Pick from the menus

Nonet reads the menus of a running app into a searchable list. Find the command and drag it onto an empty button. No need to look up shortcuts.

Three pages of nine, and a launcher

Swipe with two fingers to change pages, for up to 27 buttons. The first page is a launcher filled with apps from your Mac, a handy stand-in for Launchpad.

A tick at every edge

The trackpad gives a light tap each time your finger crosses into another button, so you know where you are without looking. Choose how strong it feels.

FAQ

Which permissions does Nonet need?

Accessibility (System Settings › Privacy & Security). Nonet uses it to detect the trigger key, to hold the pointer and clicks while you press it, to send keystrokes and to choose menu items. Reading finger positions needs no permission.

macOS asks once for Automation permission only when a script uses the browser's URL (NONET_BROWSER_URL). Running a script in Terminal doesn't ask.

Does Nonet send what I type or how I use it?

No. Nonet never records or sends what you type. Settings are stored only on your Mac (~/Library/Application Support/Nonet) and there is no account. The network is used only to check for updates and to open the purchase page.

See the privacy policy for details.

Does it work with Japanese (JIS) keyboards?

Yes. JIS keyboards have no right ⌥, so the default trigger there is right ⌘. You can also choose right ⇧ or fn.

If a tool such as Karabiner-Elements already uses a single press of right ⌘, pick a different trigger to avoid conflicts.

What are the requirements?

macOS 14 Sonoma or later and a multi-touch trackpad: the built-in trackpad of a MacBook or a Magic Trackpad. Both Apple silicon and Intel Macs are supported.

AI actions need macOS 26 or later on a Mac that supports Apple Intelligence.

Can I use it with a mouse only?

No. Buttons are chosen by where your finger rests, so you need a trackpad. A Mac mini or Mac Studio works with a Magic Trackpad.

Why isn't it on the Mac App Store?

To read finger positions while you use other apps, Nonet relies on a part of macOS that is not public. The App Store does not allow that, so Nonet is sold directly from this site. The app is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

What happens when the trial ends?

The HUD shows "Your trial has ended" and buttons no longer run. Your settings are kept, so entering a license key brings everything back immediately.

How is this different from a Stream Deck?

Stream Deck is a dedicated device from Elgato with a grid of keys that each have their own display. Nonet is software that uses your MacBook trackpad as the buttons. You need no extra hardware, but there are no physical keys or per-key screens, and the buttons exist only while you hold the trigger key.
