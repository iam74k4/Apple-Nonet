FAQ

How many Macs can I use it on?

Every Mac you personally use. Each person needs their own license.

What are the requirements?

macOS 14 or later and a MacBook trackpad or Magic Trackpad. A mouse alone won't do. AI actions also need macOS 26 or later on a Mac with Apple Intelligence.

Could a macOS update break it?

It could. Each new macOS is tested from the first beta and fixed. If it can't be fixed, you get a refund no matter when you bought it.

Tested on: list the versions you tested here

Does it send what I type?

No. Settings stay on your Mac and there is no account (privacy policy).

I can't find my license key

It's in the email sent after purchase. If you can't find it, email us with your order number.
