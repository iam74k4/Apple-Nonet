Your download has started

You're getting the latest Nonet (macOS 14 or later, for Apple silicon and Intel Macs). If nothing happens, download it here.

Open nonet.astlog.dev on your Mac to download it. To copy it to a Mac yourself, you can still download the disk image (.dmg).

Install in three steps

  1. 1. Drag Nonet to Applications

    Open the disk image you downloaded (Nonet-….dmg), then drag Nonet to the Applications folder in the window that appears.

  2. 2. Allow Accessibility (1 minute)

    Open Nonet from the Applications folder and the Welcome to Nonet window appears. Click Open System Settings, then turn on Nonet in Privacy & Security › Accessibility.

  3. 3. Slide in from the top edge

    Put a finger on the top edge of the trackpad and slide it down to open the HUD, which shows all nine buttons. Slide to choose, then lift on a button to run it. To close without running anything, slide out to the frame around the buttons and lift. You can practice in the Try It Out step of the welcome window, where nothing runs.

Your 14-day trial starts the first time you open Nonet. No card or email needed. When you're ready to buy, see Pricing.

If nothing opens, try again with one finger, sliding down from the top edge a little faster (a slow move is treated as normal pointer movement). The user guide covers the rest, and Troubleshooting helps if something doesn't work.

Nonet is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
