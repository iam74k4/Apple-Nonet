Your download has started

You're getting the latest Nonet (macOS 14 or later, for Apple silicon and Intel Macs). If nothing happens, download it here.

Open nonet.astlog.dev on your Mac to download it. To copy it to a Mac yourself, you can still download the disk image (.dmg).

Install in three steps

  1. 1. Drag Nonet to Applications

    Open the Nonet-….dmg you downloaded and drag Nonet to the Applications folder.

  2. 2. Allow Accessibility (1 minute)

    Open Nonet, click Open System Settings in the welcome window, and turn on Nonet under Accessibility.

  3. 3. Press firmly and hold

    Press firmly anywhere on the trackpad (until the second click) and hold for a moment, and nine icons open. Lift on an icon to run it, or move outside the ring of icons and lift to close without running anything.

Your 14-day trial starts the first time you open Nonet. No card or email needed.

If nothing opens, press with one finger until the second click and hold (Force Click and haptic feedback must be on in System Settings › Trackpad). See the user guide for more.

Nonet is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
